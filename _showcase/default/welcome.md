<div class="p-4">
    <h2>Outreach</h2>
    <hr />
    <p>
        My journey in science communication began in high school, around 2010, alongside Professor Carlos Argolo, helping to promote rocket launches in Alagoas. Since then, presenting and discussing science with people in a playful and relaxed way has become one of my passions.
    <p>
    <p>
        During my undergraduate studies in physics, I had the opportunity to be a monitor at Usina Ciência-UFAL thanks to Professor Antônio Ornellas, where I traveled to underprivileged communities throughout the state presenting physics experiments, operating community telescopes and planetariums, and promoting physics and astronomy education for students of all ages.
    <p>
    <p>
        Over the years, I have been actively involved in national initiatives such as the Brazilian Astronomy and Astronautics Olympiad (OBA), teacher training programs, and international collaborations in astronomy. Currently, as a qualified astronomer, I continue to seek to connect cutting-edge research in astrophysics with society through lectures, workshops, and public engagement activities.
    </p>
