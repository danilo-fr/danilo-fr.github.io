Research Interests

My research focuses on massive stars and their evolution, with particular emphasis on stellar rotation, asteroseismology, binary and hierarchical systems, and the Be phenomenon.

I am also interested in stellar variability, internal mixing processes, angular momentum transport, circumstellar disks, and exoplanets.

Research Projects

Be Stars in Binary Systems

My PhD research at the National Observatory of Brazil focused on Be stars in binary systems, investigating their properties, evolutionary pathways, and the role of stellar interactions. My current research continues to focus on Be binaries, with particular emphasis on mass transfer, binary evolution, and the physical mechanisms responsible for the formation and evolution of circumstellar disks.

Binarity at Low Metallicity (BLOeM)

As a member of the international BLOeM collaboration, I contribute to the study of massive stars in the Small Magellanic Cloud, a low-metallicity environment that offers a unique opportunity to investigate stellar evolution under conditions approaching those of the Early Universe. Using multi-epoch spectroscopy, the project aims to characterize the multiplicity properties of massive stars, constrain their binary fraction and orbital configurations, and identify candidate systems hosting dormant black holes. This work helps improve our understanding of binary interactions and the evolution of massive stars in metal-poor environments.

Binary and Hierarchical Stellar Systems

My research explores the properties, dynamics, and evolution of binary and hierarchical stellar systems. I focus on observational studies of eclipsing binaries and systems hosting Be stars, investigating stellar variability, orbital properties, and the physical processes governing interactions between stars. These studies provide important insights into binary evolution, mass exchange, and the formation and evolution of complex stellar systems.

Methods and Data

Observational Astrophysics

My research combines multi-wavelength spectroscopy and photometry across the infrared, optical, ultraviolet, and X-ray domains, together with time-domain analysis of stellar variability.

Observational Facilities and Space Missions

My observing experience includes programs with Gemini North, SOAR, and TESS, as well as participation in additional observing programs involving facilities such as VLT and OPD.

Computational Tools

I use Python, Fortran, and IRAF for scientific analysis and computational work. My interests also include spectral modeling and data-driven approaches to stellar astrophysics.
