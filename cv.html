Profile Summary

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the National Astrophysics Laboratory (LNA), Brazil. I obtained my PhD in Astronomy from the National Observatory of Brazil in 2025 and completed an internship at the Paris Observatory in 2024.

My research focuses on massive stars, Be stars, and hierarchical multiple systems. My expertise also includes stellar astrophysics, exoplanets, and astronomy education and outreach. I am a member of the BLOeM collaboration and served as a juror at IOAA 2024.
